# World board

**Place and people.** The ring (canvas, ropes, red and blue corners, the bell, the round clock), the TV truck (broadcast graphics, delay buffer), the betting floor (bookmakers on boxes with chalkboards, tic-tac men signalling prices with white gloves), the exchange (traders, ladders), the courtsider (someone in the stands with a phone, ahead of the TV).

**Visual traditions.**
1. Racecourse bookmaker chalkboards — white chalk on black slate, price in big hand-lettered fractional odds, runner names in caps, wiped and rewritten as prices move. Rules: one price per row, wipe marks visible, no colour except a red line for favourite.
2. Tic-tac signalling — white gloves, a fixed code of arm positions for each price. Rules: gesture = number, read at distance, high contrast.
3. Split-flap boards (Solari) — black flaps, white condensed caps, mechanical flip per character. Rules: fixed grid cells, every change is a flip, amber or white on black.
4. Fight posters (letterpress) — huge condensed wood type, two names stacked, red/blue or black/red on cream stock, halftone portraits. Rules: names dominate, date/venue small, heavy borders.
5. Broadcast scorebugs — hard-edged bars, corner colour chips, tabular timers, ticks of motion. Rules: always readable over action, never more than 3 numbers.
6. Betting slips/tickets — perforations, serial numbers, stamped time, carbon-copy blue. Rules: monospaced serials, stamp marks slightly rotated.
7. Ring ropes and canvas — three parallel lines, corner pads, logo stamped on canvas.

**Physical actions.** Wipe and re-chalk a price; ring the bell; flip a split-flap cell; stamp a ticket; tear a ticket stub; tic-tac signal a price; pull the ropes.

**Words people use.** Price, back, lay, odds-on, evens, the off, in-running, suspended, the bell, corner, round, KO, stoppage, tic-tac, courtsiding, delay, settled.

**Chosen art tradition:** racecourse chalkboard (+ split-flap motion for numbers). No exchange or crypto site uses chalk; it is specific to in-play pricing, and "wiped and re-chalked as prices move" is literally what our book does every tick.
**Chosen action:** wipe-and-rechalk (price updates) and stamp (fills/rejections get a stamped ticket: FILLED tick 412 / REJECTED STALE).
